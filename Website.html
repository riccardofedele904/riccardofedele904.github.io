About meChi sono

Technical depth, commercial senseCompetenza tecnica, visione commerciale

I am an aerospace engineer who likes being where the technical and the commercial meet. I recently graduated with an MSc in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Padova, and I spent seven months at MTU Maintenance Lease Services in Amsterdam working on engine asset management and evaluation across the IAE V2500 family.

It taught me how much I enjoy turning the condition of an engine into decisions a business can act on. My background covers CFD, structural analysis and composite manufacturing, and I am comfortable moving between technical detail and real-world operational calls, working with technical, commercial and legal teams in an international environment.
